WHAT CANDYLARIO DOES TODAY

The radar, on Telegram

Live since October 29, 2026. Free, no keys, no sign-up: join t.me/candylario.

What you get

Candylario watches every liquid perpetual on Hyperliquid, about a hundred coins, and posts an alert the moment one of them does something the record says is worth acting on. Each alert names the rule that raised it, tells you what happened after that kind of move on every perpetual since 2021, and says which trade that history argues for, with the stop it needs.

The record is the coin's median move over the following 24 hours and how often it was higher, on Binance's full history of USDT perpetuals, coins with at least a million dollars traded in the day. "Every year" means the direction held in each year from 2021 to 2026.

Ideas the data rejected, so you will not see them: volume or open interest without a price move, very high funding, short squeezes, buying after four green candles, and breakouts to new highs, which have faded rather than run.

A momentum run comes as a longer card: the score and its class, the inflow, the book and open interest, BTC, resistance and support, the kind of move, the record of such runs, and a verdict in plain words that ends with whether fresh money is entering.

The history line is a record, not a promise: medians over thousands of past alerts since 2021, before fees, with the last two years and the share of years that agreed shown separately, so a fading edge is visible; the past does not bind the next trade. Treat every suggestion as a starting point for your own decision.
