Risk notice

Read this before an agent trades real money.

You can lose money

Crypto prices move fast and can fall to a fraction of their value. You can lose all the money an agent trades.

The AI can be wrong

An AI model's ratings can be wrong, and results on test funds or in the past do not predict real results. Real trading has fees, slippage and thin markets that the testnet does not show.

Limits help but cannot promise

Every agent pauses for the day after a 10% loss in one day, and sells everything and stops after a 30% loss from its start. These limits reduce losses but cannot guarantee them: prices can jump past a limit, and outages at Candylario, Binance or the model provider can delay any action.

Start small

Trade only money you can afford to lose. Start with test funds, and watch your agent's decisions before you give it real money. Nothing Candylario shows you is financial advice.
