Risk notice

Read this before you trade from an alert.

You can lose money

Crypto prices move fast and can fall to a fraction of their value. Perpetual futures add leverage and funding payments, and a position can be liquidated. You can lose all the money you trade.

The record is not a promise

Each alert quotes what followed similar moves in the past. Those medians can fail for any single trade, an edge that held for years can fade, and a suggested trade can lose. Results in the past or on testnet do not predict real results.

Stops help but cannot promise

Every one-tap trade carries a stop-loss on the exchange. A stop limits losses but cannot guarantee them: prices can jump past it, it fills at the market, and thin markets slip. Outages at Candylario, Hyperliquid or Telegram can delay alerts and orders.

Start small

Trade only money you can afford to lose, in small sizes, and watch how alerts play out before you act on them. Nothing Candylario shows you is financial advice.
