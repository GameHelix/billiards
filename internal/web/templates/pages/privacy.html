Privacy Policy

In effect from October 26, 2026.

1. Who is responsible

Ismat Samadov, an individual based in the Republic of Azerbaijan, runs Candylario and is responsible for your data. Write to ismat.samadov@candy.surf about it.

2. What we keep

  • Account: your email address, a hash of your password (never the password itself), your tier, whether your address is confirmed, and delivery problems reported for it.
  • Keys: your Anthropic and Binance API keys, encrypted. We show only their last four characters.
  • Trading: your agents' settings, decisions, orders, balances and messages.
  • Telegram: if you connect a chat, its chat ID and your display name.
  • Technical: server logs with IP addresses and requests, kept for security for up to 30 days.
  • Payments: Paddle handles them. We learn the state of your subscription, never your card details.

3. Why

To run your agents, keep the service and your account safe, send you account emails (address confirmation, password resets and security notices) and bill paid tiers. We show no ads and sell no data.

4. Who else handles it

  • Hetzner Online (Germany): our servers and backups.
  • Mailjet (European Union): account emails.
  • Anthropic: your agent's briefings (market data, news and your agent's own trading) go to Anthropic under your key.
  • Binance: orders and account queries under your key.
  • Telegram: messages to the chats you connect.
  • Paddle: payments for paid tiers.
  • UptimeRobot: checks that the site is up; it sees no personal data.

5. How long

As long as your account exists. When you delete it, we delete your email, password and sessions, erase your keys and disconnect your Telegram chats; your agents' trading history stays only without your name. Encrypted backups are deleted within 30 days.

6. Security

Keys are encrypted at rest and decrypted only inside the trading engine, never by the website. Every connection uses HTTPS, and access to our servers is restricted.

7. Your rights

You can see, correct, export or delete your data, and object to how we use it. Delete your account in Settings, or write to us for anything else; we answer within 30 days. If you live in the European Union, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

8. Cookies

We use only a cookie that keeps you logged in and a short-lived one that carries a message to the next page. There are no tracking or analytics cookies.

9. Changes

We post changes to this policy here and email you about important ones.
