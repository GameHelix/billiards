A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Good questions.
Clear answers.

A little clarity before your first alert.

Is it free?+

Yes. The radar and the channel are free. When one-tap trading opens, each trade you place carries a small fee; there is no subscription.

Do I need an account?+

Not for the channel: join t.me/candylario. An account connects your own Telegram chat and, next, a wallet for one-tap trades.

Which coins does it watch?+

Every perpetual on Hyperliquid with at least a million dollars traded in the day, about a hundred coins, sampled every 30 seconds around the clock.

Are the suggestions financial advice?+

No. A suggestion is the trade the record of similar moves argues for, nothing more. The record is history, not a promise, and every trade is your own decision.

Can Candylario move my money?+

No. The agent wallet you approve can open and close positions for your account but cannot withdraw, and you can revoke it on Hyperliquid at any time.
