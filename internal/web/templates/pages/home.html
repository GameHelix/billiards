A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Good questions.
Clear answers.

A little clarity before your first agent.

Do I need real money to get started?+

No. New accounts start with Binance testnet funds and can trade BTC and ETH. Testnet balances have no real monetary value, so you can explore how your agent behaves before considering live trading.

What do I need to connect?+

An Anthropic API key and a Binance testnet API key. Setup includes instructions for both. Model calls use your Anthropic credits, so you will need to fund that API account. Telegram is optional.

How often does my agent make decisions?+

The trial tier makes scheduled decisions every four hours. Your agent may also respond to sharp market moves or major news, within the limits of your tier. You can request a decision from the dashboard.

Can I stop an agent at any time?+

Yes. You can pause trading, stop the agent while keeping its holdings, or confirm a sale of all holdings from the dashboard. Telegram also supports agent controls.

Does an AI agent guarantee a profit?+

No. Crypto markets are volatile, and an AI model can make incorrect decisions. Risk limits help constrain the strategy but cannot eliminate losses. Testnet results do not predict live performance.
